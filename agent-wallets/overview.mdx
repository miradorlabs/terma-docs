Autonomous AI agents increasingly pay for external services on-chain via x402 — search APIs, media generation, data services, and more. Rather than treating these payments as a separate concern, Terma brings them into the same attributed ledger as your token spend, sessions, and git activity. You get a single view of what every agent cost, what it touched, and whether the money moved as expected.

## How Agent Wallets Work

Each agent gets a dedicated wallet address funded from your organization's treasury. The treasury governs how wallets are topped up and how much any single wallet can hold.

- **Funding source** — all wallets draw from a shared org treasury, keeping your spend consolidated in one place.
- **Refill policy** — configure nightly, on-demand, or threshold-triggered refills so wallets stay funded without manual intervention.
- **Hard ceiling per wallet** — each wallet has a cap. When an agent reaches its ceiling, new payments are blocked until you explicitly raise the limit or approve an exception.
- **Per-payment attribution** — every x402 on-chain payment is reconciled against the exact tool call that claimed it. You can trace any charge back to a session, a principal, and a line of agent work.
- **Gap detection** — unattributed outflows (payments with no matching tool call) and claimed-vs-settled discrepancies are flagged as findings, not buried in aggregate totals.

## What Terma Does Not Do

Terma never holds private keys. Wallet metadata and settlement data flow through Terma for attribution and reconciliation; your keys remain in your own infrastructure. Terma sees what was spent and when — not the credentials that authorized it.

## Wallet Registration States

Every wallet address Terma encounters falls into one of three states:

| State | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Registered** | Explicitly added to your treasury with an owner, cap, and refill policy |
| **Inferred** | Address seen in agent traffic but not yet registered; treated as a governance finding |
| **Refill pending** | Registered wallet that has dropped below its target balance and is awaiting the next scheduled refill |

<Note>
Unregistered wallet addresses inferred from traffic appear as governance findings. Register them in Dashboard → Agent Wallets to bring them under your treasury.
</Note>

## Treasury Management

Fund wallets from the org treasury and set the policies that govern their behavior:

- **Per-wallet caps** — define the maximum balance any single wallet can hold.
- **Auto-refill target** — set the balance level that triggers a refill, and the amount to restore.
- **Approvals above cap** — any transaction that would exceed the wallet ceiling requires explicit approval before it proceeds.

This gives you budget guardrails for autonomous agents that mirror the same envelope machinery Terma uses for dev-time and run-time budgets across teams and repos.
