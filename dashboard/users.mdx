The **Users** page lists the people and API keys active in the team across every source, with their sessions, token usage, and spend in context.

## Team usage overview

The top panel summarizes the period — **Active users**, **Tokens burnt**, and **Spend** (**Net spend** in the Net view, **Output tokens** in the Tokens view), each compared with the previous period — with a chart of tokens over time by person. A **Most active** card highlights who ran the most sessions, with their share of the team's sessions and their tokens.

## People table

Search the table by name:

| Column | Description |
|---|---|
| **Person** | The person, or the API key when traffic is not tied to a person |
| **Funding** | How their usage is paid for — **Subscription**, **Prepaid credits**, or **Metered** — with the plan name when known, such as Max 20x. A pill shows the account they are on now and fills with how much of its allowance is used; **+N** opens every account they hold |
| **Tokens** | Tokens in the period |
| **Spend** | Spend in the current usage view (hidden in the Tokens view) |
| **Sessions** | Sessions in the period |
| **Skills used** | Distinct skills they invoked |
| **Last active** | Their most recent activity |

A person who uses several agents appears once when their identities are mapped to the same organization member. See [Principal mapping](/dashboard/settings#principal-mapping).

## A person's page

Select a person to open their page. It is labeled **User**, **API key**, or **Unattributed**, and the **Source** picker narrows it to one source.

- **Summary:** spend, tokens, sessions, commits, and pull requests merged.
- **Funding:** one table with a row for every account they hold, grouped by source, showing each account's plan, its spend in the period, and its allowances.
  - For a subscription, each allowance window — for example **5-hour** and **7-day** — shows how much is used, when it resets, and whether they are running fast or at the cap.
  - Prepaid credits show their balance.
  - Metered sources show **No allowance, billed at list price**.
  - A person often holds several accounts for one source. Every account gets its own row, with spend from the sessions that drew on it; a session that switched accounts counts toward the source total only.
- **Models** and **Providers:** their usage by model and by provider. Select one to narrow the page to it.
- **What they reach for:** the skills they invoked in the last 30 days, with invocations, sessions, and share.
- **Sessions:** their sessions, in the same table as the [Sessions](/dashboard/session-replay) page.

<Note>
  Allowance windows come from the plan usage your agents report: Claude Code's status line and Codex's hooks, captured once they have run `terma setup`. A person who runs an agent on a machine without it shows **unobserved** windows, and the Overview shows a funding gap banner. See [Funding and cost views](/concepts/entitlements).
</Note>
