Two pages show what your agents reach for beyond the model itself. Both follow the period selector.

## Skills

The **Skills** page ranks the skills that appear in your agent sessions by use.

- **Invocations**, **Skills used**, and **Top skill** summarize the period.
- The table lists each **Skill** with its **Invocations**, **Sessions**, and **Adoption** — the share of active people who invoked it at least once, such as **3 of 8**.

Select a skill to open its details in a dialog: its invocations and sessions, and a table of who used it, with each person's invocations, sessions, and share. The dialog has its own **Window**: `24h`, `7d`, or `30d`.

Each person's page also lists the skills they reach for most. See [Users](/dashboard/users).

## MCP

The **MCP** page shows the MCP servers your agents called, and how much.

- **MCP calls**, **Servers** reporting calls, and distinct **Tools** called summarize the period.
- The table lists each **Server** with its **Calls**, **Tools**, and **Share** of all MCP calls.

Select a server to open its details in a dialog: its calls, tools, and **Share of MCP calls**, and a table of each tool's calls and share, over its own **Window**.
